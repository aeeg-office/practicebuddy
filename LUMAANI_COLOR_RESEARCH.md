## 1. Research Basis (Phase 5–6)

### Color psychology — education & technology
- **Green** universally signals *growth, progress, safety, achievement*. In **MENA** green carries
  strong cultural resonance (growth, nature, prosperity). Perfect for the Lumaani tagline
  ("Master every skill").
- **Blue** signals *trust, competence, intelligence, success* — heavily used by universities and
  edtech (IXL, Quizlet, NWEA). It is **saturated**; too much blue risks blending into competitors.
- **Teal** (blue-green) is the **underrepresented sweet spot**: it *combines* trust (blue) with
  growth (green) and reads "technology-forward + premium". Rare in edtech — a distinctiveness win.
- **Gold/amber** conveys *premium, optimism, achievement awards* — ideal for a "mastery/award"
  accent and pairs beautifully with teal/green (moonlit-night metaphor: Lumaani = "luminous").
- **Purple** = 2025 "leadership/creative" trend but is **owned** by Khan/ALEKS/Quizlet-adjacent
  brands and Kahoot — avoid as primary.

### Competitor whitespace analysis
| Product | Primary | Whitespace for Lumaani |
|---------|---------|------------------------|
| Khan Academy | Deep blue + teal/green accents | — |
| Duolingo | Green | Green is crowded among youth apps |
| IXL | Blue | Blue crowded |
| Quizlet | Blue/indigo | Blue crowded |
| Kahoot | Purple | Purple crowded |
| NWEA | Blue | Blue crowded |
| ALEKS | Blue/purple | — |
| Kognity | Blue | Institutional blue crowded |
| Revision Village | Teal/green | Near to Lumaani's corridor |

**Conclusion:** Lumaani's **green/teal corridor is strategically correct and distinctive.**
The problem is not the hue family — it is **execution**: 5 competing legacy families
(indigo, navy, dark-navy, neon-orange, purple) fragment the brand. The work is **unification +
tone refinement**, not a hue reset.

### Multi-audience tension (the core design challenge)
Lumaani must serve **Grade 3 → adults** (parents, teachers, schools, institutional buyers).
- Too childish → loses teens/parents/institutions.
- Too corporate → loses students.
- Too dark/neon → loses all.
The **green/teal-neutral-gold system, executed calmly (Low cognitive load), with role-based
color density** (student = more accent, teacher = data-dense, admin = neutral) is the answer.

## 3. Accessibility Screening (Phase 9) — WCAG AA

All 5 directions **fully pass default + hover + link + nav + semantic** states (verified by
computation). Semantic colors use the light background + solid text convention.
Key fixes vs. the **current** system: the existing warning `#d97706` fails AA → all options use
`#a16207` (4.92:1). Option 04's bright jade was **rejected** in early screening (3.04:1 on CTA)
and re-based to `#066f5a` (6.12:1).

## 5. Recommendation (Phase 15)

**HERMES RECOMMENDATION: Option 2 — Moonlit Teal** (as the platform's spine), with a note that
**Option 5 — Deep Teal + Copper** is the strongest *distinctiveness upgrade* if the owner wants a
more premium, school-facing turn.

Why **Option 2** scores highest:
1. **It already matches the Lumaani brand/logo corridor** (`#0d4f4f`) → lowest change risk & fastest.
2. **Highest accessibility** (10/10) and **mobile suitability** (9/10).
3. **Best MENA + international balance** (9/9) and long-term flexibility.
4. Unifies the 5 stray legacy families into the **true canonical palette** — the actual fix.

> This is a **proposal only — implementation awaits owner selection.**
